  Lessons:
  1. Local opaque patches in a thin, blended sky dry paler and show. Repaint the whole band with the original piles (broad hand + blend), clipped around what must stay (`band - structure`, clip=true). Two coats about 10 days apart hide dark ghosts.
  2. A carve or patch mask must be (old paint ∩ outside the new outline). Never use a bounding rect: it buried twigs and sky (day 234).
  3. Spirit rag: use a fresh rag each pass with dip 1.0 and pressure 0.9–1. Refolding one rag drags lifted dark across the light. Wet paint over dry comes off almost completely in 10–25 passes.
  4. Twigs must grow from drawn wood. Random seeds in an annulus read as fluff. The oak() recursion worked: angular paths with occasional elbow kinks of 0.35–0.75 rad, children mostly at the elbows, the width from pressure_for(), and twig piles with medium below w 1.5.
  5. Hand-listed branch points with alternating gentle bends read as snakes. Oak needs straight runs and kinks.
  6. drying() at one point doesn't tell you about the whole path. Check several points before you wipe.
  7. Thick, light, lead-white patches took ~20 days to be touch-dry. Plan waits.
