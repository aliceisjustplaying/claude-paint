  Title: Still Life with Clay Pitcher, Quince, and Knife

  The picture achieves the quiet, contemplative gravity of a Spanish bodegón. The rustic earthenware pitcher anchors the left volume with its warm terracotta mass, turned handle, and raking glaze highlights. Beside it, the ripe golden quince sits with organic, knobby weight, catching the north light and turning into a soft umber core shadow. Across the heavy limestone table, light skims the chamfered edge of the ledge, where an old knife with turned horn handle projects forward into the viewer's space. The deep chiaroscuro background provides a resonant, silent atmosphere that unites the objects in time and light.
