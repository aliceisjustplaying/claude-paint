Watcher above the Sea of Fog

A grey dawn over an infinite cream sea of mist, seen from a dark cliff. Two slender bare oaks frame the light left and right, a tiny Rückenfigur stands at the centre of the lip, two quiet boulders with faint dry tufts lie in the turf, six birds cross the pale sky. Painted very thin over graphite and a warm ground, in the manner of Friedrich — no impasto, losses left as mist.
