**Evening Pool, After Haying**

A late-summer meadow at sundown, in the tonal manner of late Inness. A great oak mass holds the left, dark olive against a gray-violet upper sky. Two slim trees stand on the right against a lemon-gold glow, with a pale hazy sun sinking over layered violet hills. In the dusky umber foreground a small pool catches the sky, and a tiny figure in a red shawl crosses the lit far meadow.

It was built over a red-earth and umber stained ground: a rubbed umber lay-in, then opaque sky and distance, foliage stippled over dry paint, and umber-bitumen glazes to sink the foreground.

Two things are weaker than I intended. The pool's edge is crisper than the rest of the picture, and the oak's foot still meets the meadow in a fairly straight line.

The monogram at lower right was meant as a small mark but reads as "T F.", which stands for nothing.
