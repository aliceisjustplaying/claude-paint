**Title:** *Evening Tide, the Estuary*

It's a Thames-style sailing barge with russet sails, dark against the gold light just after sunset. A mauve bank of cloud crosses the sky, and a low cool shore holds a clump of trees, a windmill and one far-off sail. The barge's reflection is broken into ripples. A dark mud spit with mooring stakes and marsh grass anchors the lower left. The water is calm and gold near the horizon and gets darker and choppier towards the viewer.

The painting took a lot of correcting. One blend dragged the foresail into the sky, a glaze meant to be thin went on almost black, and a sheen on the mud turned into a pale mound. Each time I let the paint dry and painted over it, since nothing can be undone. Some of those accidents stayed: the dark glaze became the wind-ruffled foreground water.

In the last session I:
- darkened the reflection under the hull
- dulled the puddles on the mud
- broke up the mast's reflection
- added four gulls
- glazed the top of the sky and the lower-right water to keep the eye inside the picture

The top glaze came out streaky. After one soft blend I kept it as windblown cloud.
