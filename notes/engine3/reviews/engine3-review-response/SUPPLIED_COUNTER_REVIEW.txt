 What the review misses

 1. Main's released rag depends on vertex count, more than the prototype does. For the same dry line, 31.2% of the film is left with up to 17 points and 23.8%
    with 1,761; damp goes from 6.8% to 3.9% (ship_probe.log). Segments shorter than 11 mm add stamps (crates/paint/src/rag.rs:559,571), and the per-stamp shares
    compound (:416-419).
 2. What is left after the rejected quarter-rate wipe is mostly a loading ramp, not contact. The film left runs from 2.3 µm at the start to 20.2 µm at the end.
    Gross paint laid back is 6.19 µm against 8.62 µm left, and the correlation with contact is −0.29. Pickup is throttled by both whole-face load (rag.rs:418)
    and cell load (:453).
 3. A single stroke at thinner 0.75 or more never flows. Its ceiling is at or below the 2 µm wetting film, so the flow moved 0.0000% of the paint, against 16.2%
    at thinner 0.5.
 4. The look report's thinned sienna card is stale. It records 1.64 µm; the branch's final code gives 5.36 µm.

 Where the review needs pushing back

 - Work order. Packages B and C come first, but in the prototype they fix effects measured at 1% to 3% of the film. The rejected look comes from R01, R02, R09
   and the loading ramp, which sit in package D. On main the path fix is worth 7 points of film.
 - R07's retired-face ledger. A refolded face starts with held paint and no mobile paint (rag/face.rs:21-25,45-49), so retired color can never reach the canvas.
   The ledger would track nothing visible.
 - "Integrate (b)". By the report's own numbers, (b) trades the rim for a blotchy broad pass: 2.8 to 7.7 µm, and the card drops from 84.4% to 73.5%. A single
   stroke becomes a flat 3.0 µm slab.
 - Sienna neutrality. Field calls burnt sienna "richer, deeper, and more transparent than the raw earth" (§155, gutenberg.org/files/20915). A measure that
   penalizes darkness cannot express that sentence; the contrast ratio can. The reviewer's other point stands: the catalog has burnt hiding at 0.45 against raw
   at 0.40 (crates/paint/src/palette.rs:125,153).
