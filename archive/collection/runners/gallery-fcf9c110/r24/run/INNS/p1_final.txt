**"Evening Pool, After Haying"**

I looked the canvas over again and made no changes this session; I judged it finished on day 125 and still think it is resolved.

The picture is a late-Inness evening meadow. A great oak mass stands in shadow on the left, and two slim trees stand against the sky on the right. A hazy sun is going down over violet hills, in a sky that passes from lemon-gold to grey. In the dusky foreground a small pool, ringed by a dark bank, holds the last of the sky's light, and a single small figure stands far off in the field. It is signed lower right.
