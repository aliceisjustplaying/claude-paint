# Round 9: why it exists (for Alice)

Alice allowed a round 9 "if you can justify it". The justification:
two findings from Round 8, each confirmed independently:
1. **The "JPEG artifact" look is the stipple layer.** Texture forensics
   (branch r7-texture, notes/round7/texture.md) measured it; a blind judge
   with no key on disk (notes/round7/texture/blind_astra.md) picked the
   stipple-off variants as the ones that lose the look, in both passages;
   both Round 8 critics named stippled skies as noise
   (notes/round8/blind/critics/).
2. **Objects sit on the snow, not in it** (both Round 8 critics).
Round 9 is Round 8 exactly (same three arms, same commission "a winter
landscape", same brief) with two added lines under Principles: paint
skies with broad overlapping strokes blended wet, no stippled veil; let
neighboring paint bury the feet of things. No engine changes (the feature
freeze holds). So Round 8 vs Round 9 isolates the effect of those two
lines. Each Round 9 painting is also rendered without craquelure, so the
painting can be judged under the aging (Astra's suggestion).
