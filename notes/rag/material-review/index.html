Rag: three changes

Same dark paint, path, seeds, pressure 0.8 and dip 0.5. Comparisons use native-pixel crops without color adjustment.

  1. Paint-step spacing reduced from 1 mm to 0.5 mm. Contact grid refined from 49 × 49 to 97 × 97; the cloth mesh stays 19 × 19. Mechanical substeps use 0.25 mm travel so finer paint sampling does not simply double relaxation.
  2. Local static friction holds contacting points until the tangential pull exceeds a normal-contact threshold; sliding friction then allows motion. Based on Macklin et al., section 6.1. Coefficients remain estimates.
  3. Paint is stored at cloth material locations, with local loading, color, volume and cure. Picked-up paint persists between contacts, returns only where that part of the cloth touches and is deducted from the rag. Absorbed or set paint remains held but cannot smear. Refolding retires the active dirty surface.

Validation

12 focused paint and Lua/session rag tests passed; 1 diagnostic test remained ignored. This includes conservation across different pixel resolutions, spatial warm/cool carryover, refolding, drying, repeated wipes and failed-chunk rollback. Test log.

The new carryover test also ran against the previous grip prototype and failed at its first assertion: no paint survived into a separate contact. That is an expected negative control. Previous-version test log.

Still an experimental, uncalibrated coarse cloth model with flat support and no self-collision. Full release validation and visual acceptance are not complete.
