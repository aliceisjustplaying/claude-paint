### *Hünengrab im Abendlicht* (Megalithic Tomb in Evening Light)

An ancient prehistoric dolmen crowns the summit of a desolate Baltic coastal barrow (*Hünengrab*) in late autumn twilight. Prepared on a handwoven linen support with a warm knife-leveled ground and brushed lead-white surface, the landscape rises into an immense, luminous firmament that gradates from a pale amber afterglow at the horizon through silvery-buff twilight into a deep, serene cobalt and smalt blue at the zenith. In the quiet upper sky, the slender waxing crescent moon and the solitary evening star (*Hesperus*) shine serenely.

Beyond the dark, rolling ridge of the burial mound, cool evening mist pools over quiet coastal waters, with low distant headlands silhouetted against the afterglow. The megalithic tomb—two massive, rugged orthostats of Scandinavian glacial erratic granite supporting an angular, weathered capstone encrusted with pale sage and golden crustose lichen—frames a sacred, pitch-black chamber void that contemplates the passage of millennia, human mortality, and the eternal silence of the Baltic dusk. Along the crest of the tumulus, delicate bent-grass tufts and dried carline thistle skeletons sway softly in the coastal wind.
