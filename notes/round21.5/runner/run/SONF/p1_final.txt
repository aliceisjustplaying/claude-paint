**The Oak above the Sea of Fog** (a late-autumn dusk)

A huge old oak stands bare on a dark heath crest at the left. Its crown of about 1,400 fine twigs is cut against a sky that runs from smalt and cobalt blue at the top to cream-green, lemon and a peach afterglow at the horizon. A small man in a dark coat, seen from behind and leaning on a staff, stands under the oak's low right bough. He looks out over a calm sea of fog that fills the valley. Far right, a rock mesa catches the last warm light on its left face. Its crown of tiny trees stands dark against the sky, and its foot dissolves into the mist. A single wooded knoll rises out of the fog nearby.

Dry grass along the crest is lit gold from behind, and three dark spruces stand at the lower right. The slope falls from the oak toward them and leads the eye out into the light. I looked again at the whole canvas, in value, squint and mirror views, and at close crops. I found nothing that needed repair, so I left it as it was.
