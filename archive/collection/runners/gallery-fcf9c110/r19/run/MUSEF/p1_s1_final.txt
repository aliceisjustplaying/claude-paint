Morning at the Cliff's Edge

A lone Rückenfigur stands on a dark promontory between two bare young oaks, looking out over a sea of morning fog. A white bank of mist drifts in front of the stems, grey dawn clouds above a pale cream infinite, three birds aloft. Painted thinly on a warm ground — graphite underdrawing, lean washes blended and veiled, trunks drawn as tapering silhouettes — in Friedrich's quiet, contemplative manner.
