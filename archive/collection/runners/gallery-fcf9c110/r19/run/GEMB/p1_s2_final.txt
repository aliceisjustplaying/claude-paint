### *Bodegón with Clay Pitcher and Quince*

This painting is a quiet, contemplative still life in the Spanish *bodegón* tradition, evoking the austere pictorial poetry of Francisco de Zurbarán and Juan Sánchez Cotán. 

Against a resonant, deep chiaroscuro background of raw umber and bone black, a rustic earthenware pitcher stands with grounded weight upon a heavy limestone ledge, its flared neck, turned handle, and swelling terracotta belly catching raking north light with thick glaze impasto. Beside it, set slightly forward across the luminous stone plane, a ripe golden quince glows with warm autumn light, turning through an organic core shadow into delicate ambient table bounce. Light skims the chiseled front chamfer of the stone block, setting the luminous tabletop against the deep masonry shadow below, binding the forms in sacred silence, geometric balance, and time.
