# Gustave Courbet: paintings catalog
<!-- research: This catalog is reference data for research. It gives painters no guidance and stays off every painter reading list and export profile. -->

*A sourced catalog of 40 well-known paintings by Courbet. Every claim cites a
source key; the sources are listed at the end. [OBJ] means the row's own
museum object page or collection API record, given in its entry. Catalog
data (date, medium, size, accession) follows the holding museum; where the
museum's page could not be read, the entry says which record was used.
Dates, media and sizes are given in English form, height × width in cm.
Blank fields mean the value could not be verified. "Uncertain" marks
disputed or single-source points. The same data is in
`courbet_paintings.csv`.*

**Evidence levels.** *Technical study*: a laboratory or conservation
examination of that painting (X-radiography, cross-sections, analysis or a
conservation report). *Museum or witness description*: a curator's or
witness's description of how it was painted, with no examination cited.
*Catalog only*: no account of its making was found in the sources read.

Of the 40 works, 14 have technical-study evidence, 10 have a museum or witness
description and 16 have catalog data only.

### 3. The Man with a Leather Belt (L'Homme à la ceinture de cuir. Portrait de l'artiste)

### 4. The Wounded Man (L'Homme blessé)

- **Record.** https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/925 (open-access image: not established; museum page read directly) [OBJ].
- **Layers and changes.** Three successive compositions on one canvas: a woman's portrait, then the artist with his companion, then the present picture, half effacing the second ten years later [CRMF; REICHE15]. Eight or more paint layers [REICHE16]. Painted 1844 and reworked ten years later [OBJ].
- **Pigments and medium.** MA-XRF maps of Hg, Cu, As, Fe, Zn, Cr, Ba, Pb and Ca; a palette was established for each composition, but the pigments were not read (abstract only) [REICHE16].

### 5. Lovers in the Countryside (Les Amants dans la campagne, sentiments du jeune âge)

- **Record.** https://www.mba-lyon.fr/fr/fiche-oeuvre/les-amants-dans-la-campagne-sentiments-du-jeune-age (open-access image: not established; museum page read directly) [OBJ].
- **Layers and changes.** X-radiography: repentirs (the C2RMF names the Lyon 'Amants heureux'; identification with this record is an inference) [CRMF]. Several repetitions exist, which Riat calls preparations before the definitive canvas [RIAT p.36].

### 8. A Burial at Ornans (Un enterrement à Ornans)

- **Record.** https://www.musee-orsay.fr/fr/oeuvres/un-enterrement-ornans-924 (open-access image: not established; museum page read directly) [OBJ].
- **Support.** Coarse, loose canvas of three widths plus a top band; never lined; format altered [ORS-ENT].
- **Ground.** Brown preparation [ORS-ENT].
- **Knife and brush.** Generous impasto, thick paint, some applications with the knife; knife striations [ORS-ENT].
- **Layers and changes.** Preparatory drawing on paper; figures sketched in light or dark brown line; ébauche with a little lead white; landscape blocked in early; heads left in reserve; one figure moved 28 cm [ORS-ENT].
- **Pigments and medium.** X-ray fluorescence: traditional pigments with some then-recent ones, not named [ORS-ENT].
- **Condition.** Active cracks, lifting, adhesion loss; deformed 1884 stretcher; yellowed varnishes; restoration from 2025 [ORS-ENT].
