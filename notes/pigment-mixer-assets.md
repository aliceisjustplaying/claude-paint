# Fixture provenance and retained third-party terms

The two demo fixtures below are original procedural images generated by
`scripts/generate_demo_fixtures.py`. The script and its output are covered by
the repository's MIT `LICENSE`. They use no reference pictures, downloaded
datasets, external font files or Mixbox code/data.

Regenerate them with `uv run --script scripts/generate_demo_fixtures.py`.
`--check` compares regenerated bytes without changing files. The script pins
Pillow 12.3.0; exact encoded bytes depend on the encoder build as well as the
script. Fixture consumers test behavior, not these images' byte hashes.

| Fixture | Original content and test purpose | Source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `harness/delve-bot/tests/fixtures/painting.jpg` | A 1000 × 714 geometric dusk sea, retaining the mocked finished painting's dimensions and JPEG format. | Generated rectangles, lines, a polygon and a disk; project MIT. No archive painting remains in this fixture. |
| `studio/fixtures/fable-palette-board.png` | A 1000 × 690 RGB board with ten named piles across two full rows and one partial row. It retains the palette decoder's geometry, labels and thick/thin paint samples. The historical filename remains so existing consumers keep their regression coverage. | Generated geometric swatches and the project's own 3 × 5 bitmap alphabet as recognized by `studio/palette_board.py`; project MIT. No captured painter board or external font remains. |
| `crates/paint/tests/plough_volume_cure.png` | A 600 × 300 **16-bit numeric baseline**, not a reference painting. Red and green encode wet volume and cure from a seeded engine-2 ploughing test. | `crates/paint/src/bristle.rs`, `thin_and_thick` and `ploughing_films_at_the_threshold_leaves_the_recorded_cure`, identify the generator, seed 9, input field construction and original main revision `4ec3169`. Project-generated numeric data under MIT. The demo generator does not replace or weaken this exact physics baseline. |

The plough baseline compares each encoded volume/cure channel to the generated
engine fields with a tolerance of two 16-bit levels. A generic picture cannot
replace it while preserving the test. Its source construction is documented in
the test rather than inferred from its appearance.

The repository keeps its MIT spectral.js port and MIT Watercolor Lab port with
their upstream notices. Those grants do not establish permissions for unrelated
measured spectra or future dataset downloads. Original simulator parameters
remain identified as estimates rather than measured pigment spectra.

The following third-party components remain under their own terms, not MIT:

- Studio StyLua 2.5.2 is MPL-2.0. Preserve the license and covered-source
  availability when distributing its WASM. The tagged source is linked in
  `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
- The Python MCP dependency closure includes certifi's Mozilla CA bundle under
  MPL-2.0. Preserve its license and data provenance when bundling it; Python
  inline requirements are not a complete deployed environment inventory.
- Watercolor Lab's Figtree fonts are SIL OFL-1.1, with the license beside the
  fonts. Commercial embedding is allowed, but standalone sale and font
  relicensing are restricted. The font terms do not apply to rendered output.
- FFmpeg/libx264 is a separate invoked executable. A GPL-enabled FFmpeg build
  remains GPL; copying it into a distribution carries its own source/notice
  obligations. This does not make its output pictures or videos GPL.

Research snippets under `notes/` remain with their source list. Their manifest
pins the selected source passages but is not a uniform third-party license
grant. The STIL-212 license audit records every reference-library selection,
its source file and line range; no reference snippets are deleted or rewritten
by this fixture replacement.
