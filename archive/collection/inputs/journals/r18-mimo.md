- day 2, 10:16: Subject: a white stoneware jug with two lemons on a table, dark wall behind. Light from the upper left. Ground is a warm ochre imprimatura (lead white + yellow ochre, knife, then a brushed warm tan) so the warm underlayer unifies everything.
- day 2, 10:16: Blocked the wall (raw umber + bone black + red earth) and the table (yellow ochre + red earth + lead white) with broad strokes, then filled. Learned early that work() leaves gaps between strokes unless coverage is high and fill=true; and that blending a lean layer twice lifts paint off the weave and leaves a pale lattice.
- day 2, 10:16: Cast shadows first (ribbons + contact ellipses), then the objects over them. The first shadow pass with short scumble strokes read as dark worms - too contrasty. Fixed later with transparent glazes of raw umber over the whole shadow shape, which is how a shadow should be laid anyway.
- day 2, 10:16: Jug: silhouette as a smoothed poly plus a ribbon handle. First block-in was full of holes (short strokes, dry runs) - re-covered at coverage 2.2 with clip=true, which also cut a crisp contour. Rendering done with glazes over the white body: smalt+raw umber for the shadow side, a darker core band where the form turns, a warm lead-white/yellow-ochre glaze for reflected light along the right edge, opaque warm white on the lit side. The form lighting is a diagonal terminator, hand-placed rather than derived from a solid, so the line could be softened by hand where it read as a facet.
- day 2, 10:16: Lemons: chrome yellow and yellow ochre, olive shadows (yellow ochre + green earth + red earth), specular touches of chrome yellow + lead white. A dark crease ribbon where the two fruits touch is what separates them; without it they read as one yellow smear. Green earth leaf at the right for a cool accent.
- day 2, 10:16: Background: three quiet fields - warm dark wall with a glow at the upper left where the light comes from, deep dark at the right, warm table with a pool of light at the front left. Everything unifies with glazes at medium 0.55-0.62, which is what finally killed the speckle from earlier scumble passes.
