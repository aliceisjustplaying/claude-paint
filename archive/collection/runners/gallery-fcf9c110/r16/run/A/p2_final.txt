- **Title:** *Hünengrab im Schnee am Abend* (Dolmen in Snow at Evening)

A stag-headed oak with dead silver limbs stands over a snow-covered Stone Age tomb (a dolmen) on a low mound. A small figure in a long coat, seen from behind, has walked out across the field; his footprints lead back toward us. The sky shades from cold smalt-grey at the top to a rose-ochre glow at the horizon, with a thin crescent moon and a few crows. A village church and a group of snowy spruces sit low on the right. I left the band of glowing sky above the far woods empty on purpose, since that open space carries most of the Friedrich mood.

In the last stretch:
- **Foreground:** I laid a very thin cool veil over it, so the snow darkens toward the viewer as the light fades.
- **Footprints:** I shaded their far edges so they read as hollows rather than pale discs.
- **Oak:** I painted out a burr on the trunk that looked like a button.
- **Varnish:** I finished with a light coat once everything was dry.

Two faults remain:
- **Faint streaks:** the first test glaze (bottom-right corner) came out far too strong. I lifted it off and repainted, but a few faint marks may still show at the extreme bottom-right edge.
- **Limb snow:** it's still a little bright, though at full size it reads as thin frost.
