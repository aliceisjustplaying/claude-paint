# easel3_free: Dolmen on the Shore at Evening

Started 13:13 (BST), 2026-09-23.

## The picture and why

A Hünengrab (a megalithic dolmen: great boulders with a capstone) on a low
dune knoll above the Baltic, just after sunset. A clear afterglow sky: warm
yellow at the sea horizon, through a pale green-gray band into cool violet
blue. A thin crescent moon and the evening star. Two old oaks, one dead,
stand bare behind the stones; a single figure in a dark coat, seen from
behind (the Rückenfigur), stands near the stones looking out to sea. The sea
is calm and dim, with one or two small sails. Foreground: dune grass, heather
and scattered stones, painted last in fine upturned strokes.

What it draws on (from knowledge, no pictures): Friedrich's dolmen subjects
(the Hünengrab paintings of 1807 and c.1820), his Rügen and Greifswald coast
evenings, his moonrise-over-the-sea pictures and the crescent moons and
evening stars in his late skies, the Rückenfigur, dead oaks as
memento mori, a low horizon under a large quiet sky, and a thin, stippled
sky over a light ground (notes/research/friedrich_materials.md).
