**Title: *Self-Portrait with Palette***

A half-length self-portrait, facing the viewer as in a mirror, set against a warm broken-color halo that swells out of a dark umber-and-black ground like lamplight or the turbulence of the paint itself. The coat is a near-black warm-cool weave of raw umber, bone black, and a touch of Prussian blue. Held low in front is a kidney palette bearing seven dabs of the actual pigments used — white, ochre, vermilion, red earth, cobalt, green earth, bone black — a mixed grey smear, a thumb through the hole, and three brush handles fanned up over the shoulder. The likeness is plain and direct, more mood and atmosphere than exact resemblance — fitting, I think, for a painter working without a mirror or a photograph, only a memory of a face.
