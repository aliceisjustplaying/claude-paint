Thin-film flow experiment (reverted in 4e30c114)

Top three lines: single strokes thinned with half, three quarters and nine tenths spirits. Below: a broad wash thinned half. The old candidate retained a 2 µm floor; the experimental candidate allowed thinner films to move slowly. These are historical images, not the current implementation.

The difference is hard to see: on average 0.35 grey levels out of 255, at most 10. The change matters mostly so that very thin washes aren't frozen in place.
