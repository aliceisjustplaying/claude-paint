- **Title:** *Low Water, Evening*

It's an estuary at low tide in the evening, on a canvas 600 mm wide. A tarred boat with a red strake lies beached on dark mud, tied by a rope to a stake. A pale channel curves back past a line of old posts towards a low gold glow on the horizon. A church tower and a dark band of trees give the distance, and a heavy lilac cloud cover closes the top. The dark foreground wedge and the light channel carry the picture, and the lighter sky sits above them.

The painting was already saved as finished when I came back to it. Looking again, I thought the near mud was too flat and empty and the bottom edge didn't hold the picture in. I did one more short session:
- **Mud:** a thin umber-black glaze over the lower mud, getting darker toward the bottom edge.
- **Puddles:** four small puddles in the mud. At first they came out as bright chalky patches, so I glazed them down, broke up their edges and added a short gleam of sky light. They now read as wet hollows at full size.

A few flaws remain. Where I roughened the puddle edges, faint lighter rings were left in the mud. There's still a faint rectangular repaint by the stake, and the upper clouds are a little soft.
