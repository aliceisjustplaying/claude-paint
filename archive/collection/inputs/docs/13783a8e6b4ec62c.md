**Lessons learned**:
- Lead white dominates mixtures: for mid or dark tones use little white.
- Thin films come out lighter and warmer, so dark lines need a black-rich pile.
- **Never paint dark over wet light or light over wet dark.** The result goes muddy grey. Wait (often 3–8 days of painting time with `wait`) and check with `drying(x,y)` before overlapping. Tacky surfaces grab.
- The detail hand with `load=1, pressure={0.6,0.9}, coverage 3–4` gives opaque fills.
- A `rect` clip on a glaze leaves a visible seam.
