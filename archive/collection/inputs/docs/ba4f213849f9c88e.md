**Lessons learned:**
- Lead white dominates mixes, so pale piles come out even paler.
- Thresholded noise patches of different piles look like camouflage or bricks. Don't use them.
- Stipples into wet paint fuse. Model on a dry layer.
- Blending near wet neighbors drags them (it smeared the figure, the far line and the copse). Never blend across dry water with a wet band.
- Blend-mask edges leave seams.
- Glazes over dry paint are weak unless medium is about 0.6.
- Painting pale over wet dark (and the reverse) lifts and streaks, so wait for paint to dry.
- `ribbon` widths are full widths.
- `bone black` mixes need 3 or more days to dry.
