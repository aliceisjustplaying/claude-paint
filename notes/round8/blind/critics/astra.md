## 3. Advice, highest payoff first

1. **Make the emotional relationship dominant.** X: preserve the figure-cross interval. Y: simplify the stream and remove some foreground incident. W: preserve the disproportion between traveler and tree. Z: quiet the right tree so the figure and arch can hold attention. These changes address the competing shapes visible in the wholes. (`X.png`, `Y.png`, `W.png`, `Z.png`)

2. **Try a version with no crack effect.** Judge the underlying painting before adding any surface treatment. The networks are particularly distracting in X and Z; Y already has useful directional marks underneath its mesh. Tool builders: make aging independently switchable, not a prerequisite for a painterly finish. (`X_crop_center.png`, `Z_crop_center.png`, `Y_crop_upperright.png`)

3. **Paint fewer branches with more consequential strokes.** Replace spike fans, tiered fir units and hairline twig webs with a few unequal masses, then add only selected branches. Tool builders: prioritize pressure changes, paint depletion and bristles merging into one loaded stroke over additional fine lines. (`X_crop_lowerleft.png`, `W_crop_lowerleft.png`, `Z_crop_upperright.png`)

4. **Let neighboring paint alter objects.** Drag snow across trunk feet, scumble atmosphere across distant edges and let some branches disappear into sky. Keep selected hard edges rather than roughening everything equally. The partly buried ruin in Y suggests the direction; X's pollard feet and Z's grave markers need it. Tool builders: make pickup and overpainting easy to control locally. (`Y_crop_center.png`, `X_crop_lowerleft.png`, `Z_crop_lowerleft.png`)

5. **Replace smooth snow modeling with broad, imperfect mixtures.** Carry cool shadow color into light snow, leave some strokes unblended and vary their direction with the ground. Use Y's overlapping snow marks as the starting point, not the silky finish in W. Tool builders: allow a brush to retain several colors through a stroke rather than immediately averaging them. (`Y_crop_lowerleft.png`, `W_crop_lowerleft.png`)

6. **Stop before every object is explained.** Leave gaps in Y's stream, reduce W's decorative grass and omit some of Z's twig detail. More texture would not fix those passages; fewer marks with clearer purpose would. (`Y.png`, `W.png`, `Z_crop_upperright.png`)
