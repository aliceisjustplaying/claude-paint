- **Title:** *June Morning on the Meadows before the Town*

This is a wide summer morning on flat Pomeranian river meadows, with a low horizon and a sky running from deep cobalt at the top to warm cream at the horizon. On a grassy rise at the left stands a lone oak in full leaf, with one dead limb breaking out of its crown. Under it, a man in a dark coat and a woman in a wine-red dress stand with their backs to us, looking toward a Hanseatic town across the meadows. The town is a violet-grey silhouette: a tall hall church with a needle spire and a few other towers, set against a large summer cloud. A river of pale reflected sky winds toward the town between pollard willows.

The meadows are full of small scenes. On the left a shepherd leans on his crook beside his dog and flock. Haymakers load a wagon behind a bay horse among the hay cocks. Two horses graze, cattle stand by a fence along the ditch, and a barge with a red-brown sail heads up the river toward the town. There are field stones and buttercups in the near grass, and swifts high in the sky.

In this last session I added the barge, the flock with its shepherd and the hay wagon. I also removed the pale halos around the towers. Patching them locally only made ghostly spires, so I repainted the whole cloud and then glazed the town so it reads as a separate shape in front of the cloud.

Two weaknesses remain. The right side of the oak's crown is still a heavy, rather even dark. The cloud is soft rather than glowing, and streaky seen close up.
