### About the Picture

Painted in oil on a plain-weave linen canvas prepared with a warm earth imprimatura, this still life brings together everyday domestic elements within a quiet chiaroscuro atmosphere inspired by the 17th-century Spanish *bodegón* tradition of Zurbarán and Cotán. 

Arranged across an aged oak tabletop, the composition balances weight, texture, and light:
- At the center stands an earthenware pitcher with an ample turned body, modeled in rich terracotta earth, umber core shadows, and warm olive-amber glazes that capture specular lights on the shoulder and lip.
- Beside it, a ripe whole lemon and a cut half introduce vibrant chrome and ochre brilliance against the deep background; the cut fruit reveals glistening radial segments of translucent pulp, pale pith, and a spiraling peel curling outward over the chamfered table edge.
- A fresh botanical lemon leaf with its woody stem bridges the citrus to a starched white linen napkin folded over the tabletop and cascading downward into the foreground.
- To the right, a solitary walnut with its rugged shell grounds the lateral balance of the light and cast shadows.
