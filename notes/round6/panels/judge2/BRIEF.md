way the paint was applied differs. You don't know who made them or how;
don't guess. Look only at the images in this folder; don't open other files.

1. set1: a broadleaf tree crown in summer leaf
2. set2: a bare winter tree
Files: setN_P.jpg, setN_P_crop.jpg (and Q, R): a 1000px view of the whole
and one crop from a 3200px render (same window within a set). Look at every
image carefully, whole and crop, before writing.

The one question that matters: does it look good as a painting? Judge as a
painter would: masses and values, edges (found, soft, lost), how the
subject meets its surroundings, economy of marks, how the paint surface
reads (anything mechanical, repetitive or digital-looking).

For each set write:
```
setN ranking: best > middle > worst (e.g. Q > P > R), margin: slight|clear|strong
why: <two or three sentences>
best's remaining defects: <the two or three most costly, concrete: what, where, why>
```

text. Be concrete and plain; no scores.
