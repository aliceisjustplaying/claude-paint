- day 26, 17:29: Mistake turned gift — the smeared veil dried as thin mist banks behind the recut trunks. Trees stand dark again. Now fine terminal twigs and foreground life: stones, dry grasses, a trodden path to the figure.
- day 36, 03:16: Mist banks drifted in front of the stems — kept, as fog does. The middle is now an infinite cream void; it needs only birds to breathe. Small dry touches only from here, no more broad reworking.
