# Oil-film mechanics: numerical contract and parameter provenance

This is the numerical mechanics module for STIL-322, not a calibrated artist-oil material law. The implementation is `crates/paint/src/oil/mechanics.rs` and its `mechanics/` submodules. Its input is an existing retained physical patch. It does not generate a height texture, choose a seed wavelength, own pigment or advance chemistry. The source audit is Captain Drive `handoffs/STIL-322/mechanics-audit.md`; experimental evidence and its limits are in `reports/STIL-312.md` and `handoffs/STIL-312/sources.md`.

No build or test runs on the fresh implementation machine. The parent subsequently authorized standalone `rustc` verification on its primary machine, only under `/home/user/.capy/work/STIL-322-mechanics-debug/`. The measured receipts below come from that location, not the fresh machine or the live checkout. An algorithm from a convergent element family is still not measured h-convergence of this implementation; the coupled nonconforming plate/fluid trace and resolution errors need their own witnesses.

## Material and stress convention

All quantities are SI and f64. Stress resultants are tensile-positive. Engineering-shear vectors are `e=[e_xx,e_yy,2e_xy]`, `k=[k_xx,k_yy,2k_xy]`, `T=[T_xx,T_yy,T_xy]`, `M=[M_xx,M_yy,M_xy]`. The plane-stress matrix is

`Q=E/(1-nu^2) [[1,nu,0],[nu,1,0],[0,0,(1-nu)/2]]`.

For cell thickness h and midpoint distance z from a specified reference surface,

`A += h Q`, `B += h z Q`, `D += (h z^2 + h^3/12) Q`.

The cubic term is exact within a piecewise-constant cell, including a one-cell skin. Different Poisson ratios retain the full A/B/D block. No averaged modulus, whole-packet coherency threshold or neutral-axis assumption is substituted for this integral.

The parent supplies already integrated **post-birth** chemical force and moment increments. A new load-bearing cohort starts stress-free at its current metric and curvature. Increasing tangent stiffness does not rescale retained force or moment. For a homogeneous-relaxation cohort, the frozen-step drive is

`T_drive=exp(-dt/tau) T_old - dT_chemical`, `M_drive=exp(-dt/tau) M_old - dM_chemical`.

Then `T_new=T_drive+A de+B dk`, `M_new=M_drive+B^T de+D dk`. Positive restrained free expansion therefore creates negative tensile force, or compression. Restrained shrinkage creates tension. Separate depth cohorts are necessary when relaxation/source histories differ; averaging their histories is not an exact constitutive reduction. The caller owns that choice. Coincident bonded cohorts use distinct packet/cohort IDs but identical physical triangle vertices: they share both fluid trace velocities and physical edge-rotation unknowns, so summing their energy is a compatible laminate, not independently wrinkling sheets. Their incoming retained edge slopes must agree. A moment remapped to a different depth reference also needs `M_new=M_old+(z_ref_old-z_ref_new) T`; the host cannot transport moments as reference-independent scalars. The chemical increment must already include any desired within-step Maxwell source weighting; the solver does not infer it from oxidation or a stage label.

## One coupled finite-depth variational problem

The gap mesh consists of positive-volume, conforming tetrahedra. Adjacent material cells share a face, not two independently evaluated boundaries. A skin is a conforming triangular material interface whose vertices are the same velocity vertices as the fluid on either side. Several skins and all their gaps are assembled into one system. A supported coherent sheet may have no mobile cells; its vertices and bending modes are fixed. Coherent material volumes remain separate from fluid tetrahedron inventories.

The velocity/pressure pair is the first-order Bernardi–Raugel/P0 element: three continuous P1 vertex velocity components and one normal cubic face bubble per unconstrained interior face, with one constant pressure constraint per tetrahedron. The face bubble is `60 lambda_i lambda_j lambda_k n`, normalized to unit face-average normal velocity. There are 16 velocity degrees of freedom on an unrestricted tetrahedron. The divergence constraint is the exact volume-average divergence, including the bubble face integral. The velocity is weakly incompressible, not pointwise divergence-free: diagnostics report both the constrained cell average and the unconstrained quadrature maximum. Pointwise incompressibility improves by mesh refinement and is not represented as a solved exact condition.

Element source: Bernardi and Raugel, *Analysis of Some Finite Elements for the Stokes Problem*, Math. Comp. 44 (1985), DOI [10.1090/S0025-5718-1985-0771031-7](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1985-0771031-7). The definition and DOFs were independently read in [DefElement, Bernardi–Raugel](https://defelement.org/elements/bernardi-raugel.html). No added numerical viscosity or pressure stabilization is used. Boundary/interface normal bubbles are constrained so the fluid trace there is P1. This trace restriction converges under refinement but is not a claim of exact finite-mesh pointwise coupling to the quadratic bending interpolant.

Plate bending uses the quadratic nonconforming Morley element: vertex heights and a shared normal-slope degree of freedom on each edge. The six local polynomial interpolation conditions are inverted from the actual triangle coordinates. Curvature is the polynomial Hessian, not an arbitrary recovered Laplacian. Normal slopes are retained in `BendingMode` and updated on accepted steps; they are not reconstructed from a coarse height image after every wait. Membrane geometry uses the P1 shared material trace. This is a thin-skin, small-slope plate discretization; finite-thickness Kirchhoff surface-rotation offsets at the two faces of a skin are not separately resolved. That approximation is distinct from the **unreduced three-dimensional** Stokes problem in the gaps.

Morley element reference: L. S. D. Morley, *The triangular equilibrium element in the solution of plate bending problems*, Aero. Quart. 19 (1968), 149–169. The definition was read in [DefElement, Morley](https://defelement.org/elements/morley.html): vertex values and edge-midpoint normal derivatives for a scalar quadratic triangle. The nonlinear mixed trace assembly here still needs the retained h/h2 numerical witness; this note does not promote the element reference into an implementation proof.

For current surface slope g and displacement increment `(u,w)`, the metric increment is

`de=sym(grad u)+[grad(w) tensor g + g tensor grad(w)+grad(w) tensor grad(w)]/2`.

The last term consumes compressive slack as relief grows. It is evaluated locally and differentiated in both the residual and Hessian. There is no patch-mean saturation closure or amplitude clamp. With curvature increment dk, the incremental plate potential per projected area is

`T_drive:de+M_drive:dk + de:A:de/2 + de:B:dk + dk:D:dk/2`.

Only exposed surfaces contribute oil/air capillary energy, `sigma |grad h|^2/2`. There is no invented Winkler spring. Differentiating the same energy supplies `div div M-div(T grad h)` and the tangential `-div T` load. Tension is stabilizing, not a signed wrinkle multiplier.

The full incremental objective is this surface potential plus

`dt integral [eta D_dev(v):D_dev(v) + tau_y sqrt(2 D_dev(v):D_dev(v))] dV`, subject to cell-volume divergence constraints. `D_dev=sym(grad v)-I div(v)/3`; the yield criterion never counts pressure or a finite-mesh volumetric strain error as shear. At the incompressible limit D_dev=D. The independent full divergence is still measured, not concealed by this deviatoric projection.

This is finite-depth creeping flow, including vertical velocity, deviatoric normal strain and shear. It never switches to lubrication when qH is large. At every positive-weight quadrature point the augmented-Lagrangian split `z=D_dev(v)` has the exact Bingham proximal update

`z = rho/(rho+2 eta) max(0,1-sqrt(2) tau_y/(rho |D_dev(v)+y|)) (D_dev(v)+y)`.

The scaled dual gives the actual symmetric deviatoric stress `rho y`. The zero branch is a yielded/unyielded active set, not a mobility gate. It retains a finite admissible stress at exactly zero strain rate. The pressure dual constrains cell-mean divergence. The velocity minimization uses an analytic Newton Hessian, incomplete-Cholesky preconditioned CG and deterministic Armijo backtracking. If incomplete Cholesky has a nonpositive pivot, only its preconditioner falls back to the diagonal; the physical operator is not shifted. Nonpositive Newton curvature falls back to a descent direction; an unconverged solve is never accepted. Nonconvex compression is handled by continuation from zero increment, step subdivision and actual full/two-half-state comparison, not by asserting global minimization of an indefinite energy.

The constant augmented-Stokes Gram matrix is assembled once per physical mesh and reused throughout the active set. Only the small sheet Hessian is updated in Newton. The line search evaluates its **increment**, not subtraction of large augmented energies: `Delta F = alpha grad(F).d + alpha^2 dt rho d.K.d/2 + elastic_nonlinear_remainder`. The elastic remainder includes the exact quadratic metric change and A/B/D terms. This eliminates an absolute-energy cancellation that stalled the initial implementation near convergence without relaxing a force target. The fused dual-force recurrence is `Z=D^T W z`, `L_new=L_old+K v-Z_new`, `rhs_new=Z_new-L_new`, with K including the cell-divergence Gram block. Final acceptance and every failure receipt recompute the physical stress/pressure force directly from quadrature, so accumulated arithmetic in this recurrence cannot replace the acceptance residual.

An arrested candidate is explicitly tested at **exactly zero velocity** against the stress-ball and force-balance residuals. An approximate tiny unyielded velocity is not accepted as years of numerical creep. The actual caller tolerances still bound static force uncertainty. Plastic and viscous dissipation are evaluated from the actual velocity field as `dt tau_y sqrt(2 D:D)` and `2 dt eta D:D`.

## Ledger, geometry and the transaction boundary

`MechanicsTrial` contains the complete shared-face fluid transfers, separate skin-edge transfers, next force/moment, retained bending modes and material displacements. For an interior fluid face the signed volume is `dt area (mean(v_vertices) dot n + bubble_velocity)`. Its positive direction is the first adjacent tetrahedron toward the second, not increasing material ID. Faces inside one material-control-volume ID cancel and need not be emitted. Faces occupied by coherent material cannot transport gap material through that skin. Skin mass moves using its own tangential trace velocity and donor thickness, not gap-average velocity. Totals are gathered in stable face order; excess outgoing donor volume subdivides the step rather than clipping pigment or thickness.

The material authority implements `ConservativeRemap::remap(source, trial)`. This operation is **provisional and side-effect-free with respect to canonical state**, because the solver invokes it independently for a full step, its first half and its second half. It carries every extensive inventory and the corresponding stress/history carriers, rebuilds actual ordered geometry and returns only its mechanical view in `RemappedPatch`. The host retains ownership of the associated provisional material snapshot and commits the accepted full-step snapshot atomically. A callback that mutates the canonical store on its first invocation violates this contract.

Eulerian graph height changes by `dw_material-grad(h) dot du_material`; material displacement is not a height overlay. The solver compares remapped interface geometry with that kinematic prediction and checks material-cell geometric volumes against the shared-face ledger. Actual discrepancies appear in `kinematic_error_m` and `volume_geometry_error_m` with explicit physical tolerances. Geometry or topology that cannot represent that update requests refinement. It does not discard the ledger and retain only displacement. Reordered/remeshed topology within a proposal is rejected for refinement; conservative refinement belongs between canonical proposals.

`controls::UniformFixtureRemap` is a deliberately restricted homogeneous fixture callback. It applies the graph kinematic update, advects force and moment with skin volume and updates the homogeneous skin tangent. It has no pigment store and is not a substitute for the production authority. The caller's remapper must also perform objective history transport when physical in-plane rotation matters; this small-strain module does not rotate separately stored pigment/packet records on the caller's behalf.

`attempt_step` accepts only after both the full step and two half steps converge and are remapped. It compares their actual geometry plus retained forces and moments. Under the frozen-rate source assumption the Maxwell-weighted half-step chemical increment is the full increment divided by `1+exp(-dt/(2 tau))`, reducing to one half when tau is infinite. This makes a restrained homogeneous constant-rate source consistent under exact Maxwell relaxation; blindly halving an already relaxation-weighted increment would not. Nonconstant chemical rates are the outer chemistry integrator's error-control problem, not inferred from a single supplied increment. The solver returns the full-step ledger and full remapped mechanical state; the half-step states are error-estimator branches, never additional committed material moves. Endpoints and interval choice remain entirely with the canonical scheduler. Nonconvergence, donor depletion and excessive error consume no time. Energy diagnostics are an **incremental potential difference with the frozen drive**, not a guessed absolute accumulated strain energy or a chemical-energy budget. Relaxation and chemical work are not falsely labeled as conserved mechanical energy.

Support vertices are no-slip. Physical wall vertices are also no-slip in this initial boundary implementation, stronger than merely no outflow; a free-slip physical sidewall requires an additional constraint implementation rather than silently reusing this one. Artificial collars are fixed only as a truncation device and their computed reactions must be below the traction tolerance. Otherwise `ExpandDomain` is returned. Storage-tile edges are ordinary interior faces, never periodic seams. Positive material-cell volumes, positive deformed tetrahedron volumes and substrate inequalities are checked; there is no substrate-penetration correction that deletes paint. Finite folds, contact topology changes, delamination and fracture remain outside the small-slope intact-interface model and require explicit failure/refinement rather than hidden clipping.

## Analytical references and numerical choices

The homogeneous traction-free normal Stokes oracle is

`M(q)=[sinh(2qH)-2qH]/[2 eta q (cosh(2qH)+2(qH)^2+1)]`.

The separate **coupled** oracle retains normal/longitudinal displacement. With u proportional to sin(qx), w proportional to cos(qx), a=qH, s=sinh(a) and c=cosh(a), solving `w''''-2q^2 w''+q^4 w=0`, `w(0)=w'(0)=0` gives

`[U,W] = [[sc+a,-a^2],[-a^2,sc-a]] [t_long,t_normal] / [2 eta q(c^2+a^2)]`.

This matrix is derived directly from the Stokes equations: a basis satisfying the lower no-slip condition is `sinh(qz)-qz cosh(qz)` and `qz sinh(qz)`. Applying the upper shear and normal tractions gives the stated matrix. Its thin limits are `M_UU=H/eta`, `M_UW=-q H^2/(2eta)`, `M_WW=q^2 H^3/(3eta)`. Constraining U=0 takes the Schur complement and recovers `q^2 H^3/(12eta)`. In deep fluid its diagonal entries both approach `1/(2eta q)` and its off-diagonal entries approach zero. The coupled growth oracle computes the two eigenvalues of `-M H_energy`, where `H_energy=[[A_xx q^2,-B_xx q^3],[-B_xx q^3,D_xx q^4+(T_xx+sigma)q^2]]`. This is a longitudinal Fourier reference for a homogeneous flat laminate with its stated in-plane constraint, not independent rendered Fourier wrinkles. `traction_free_linear_growth_oracle` is explicitly named to distinguish the scalar reference from this coupled-skin oracle. Eigenvalue evaluation uses the determinant to avoid subtracting nearly equal numbers for a slow mode.

For qH<0.01 the implementation uses `H(qH)^2/(3eta) [1-9(qH)^2/5+23(qH)^4/7]`; the switch is a proposed numerical roundoff/truncation choice, not a material constant. Otherwise exponentials are scaled by `exp(-2qH)`, so impasto/deep limits do not overflow. The limits are `H^3 q^2/(3eta)` and `1/(2eta q)`. This scalar oracle applies to a traction-free surface only. It is kept separate from the elastic-skin coupled eigenproblem and the finite-amplitude yield test. The growth oracle multiplies it by the actual homogeneous energy curvature `Bq^4+(T+sigma)q^2`; it never tests yield by `N/H`.

The thin-gap Bingham reference uses `Y=tau_y/(H |grad p|)` for a free top and `Y=2 tau_y/(H |grad p|)` for an immobilized top, with shape factor `1-3Y/2+Y^3/2`, zero at Y>=1. The Newtonian coefficients are respectively 1/3 and 1/12. These are reference identities, not the production finite-depth transport core.

Volume quadrature is tensor Gauss–Legendre under a Duffy tetrahedron map. The user chooses order 3–8; proposed order 4 has positive weights and integrates the polynomial viscous terms exactly. Bingham norm quadrature is not polynomial-exact, so depth/lateral/quadrature refinement still matters near a yield front. The Gauss root solve uses at most 32 Newton iterations and a four-machine-epsilon stopping threshold. Those are mathematical algorithm limits. Bubble normalization 60, shear factors sqrt(2), within-cell 1/12 and Duffy Jacobian 6 are exact polynomial/tensor identities, not fitted parameters.

Newton CG stops its relative squared residual at 1e-4. Armijo sufficient decrease is 1e-4; backtracking halves a step at most 64 times. The inner force target is one tenth of the requested outer traction error. These are explicitly proposed numerical convergence parameters. Exhaustion requests subdivision; none causes an accepted truncated physical interval. Scratch arrays and quadrature-row capacities are reused between calls, while provisional states and the active patch mesh remain local allocations. No performance or worst-case memory claim has been measured.

## Controls and provenance of the fixture numbers

`controls::CONTROLS` contains the growing positive plus the seven requested interventions. The primary positive is finite relief with finite yield. The scratch runner's `linear` argument instead sets zero yield and a 1 nm seed, specifically to isolate infinitesimal growth. One is not evidence for the other. Existing relief is the physical fixture geometry with its material volume already present; no call re-seeds a patch or adds an unaccounted signed displacement.

Every parameter in `proposed_parameters()` is explicitly a **proposed/unfitted numerical fixture**, not a measured oil calibration, except these source-constrained choices: 75 Pa is the reported wrinkling-specimen yield stress in Ranquet et al., S1; 12 micrometers is STIL-312's through-coherent thin-film control geometry, not a universal exemption. The fixture chooses a 0.4 mm square, 150 micrometer gap, 25 micrometer skin, E=2 MPa, nu=0.45, eta=100,000 Pa s, sigma=0.03 N/m, expansive strain=0.04 and 3 micrometer existing relief with `sin^2(pi cx x/L) sin^2(pi cy y/L)`, initially cx=cy=1. This seed is smooth and has zero edge slope without multiplying by an unresolved higher-frequency envelope. None of those latter choices is fitted to S1. The 100,000 Pa below-yield intervention is a proposed numerical arrest fixture; its actual admissibility must be established by the computed stress residual, not its label. The positive grid is initially 8x8x3 purely for a compact diagnostic, not evidence of resolved wavelength/depth convergence. Mobile fixtures request refinement below eight samples per seed cycle. The runner overrides its lateral grid and depth to two cells for measurement convenience and prints the resulting outcome without asserting growth.

The first parent compilation found that a 2x2 no-flux patch had only one free height vertex, so volume conservation forbade any height change. A 3x3 seed sampled four identical raised interior values, likewise exciting the constrained plateau instead of a resolved wrinkle. Those cases cannot witness positive growth. The corrected flowing table uses at least eight samples per seed cycle. It demands height changes larger than its actual step-doubling error plus 1e-8 of the physical seed amplitude, an explicitly proposed observability floor that rejects floating-point noise rather than relaxing the physics residuals. The parent's first six-test run also found an unchanged-history multiply/divide roundtrip in the homogeneous fixture remapper; untouched histories now pass through without arithmetic. These are verification findings from the parent, not tests rerun on this machine.

The subcritical fixture uses `N=0.9 sigma`, a sufficient domain-independent positive-curvature bound, not an estimate of the actual finite-domain buckling threshold. No-compression, tension and subcritical sign controls use zero yield to demonstrate nonzero mobility. Solid bulk and the 12 micrometer through-coherent mechanical fixture keep their geometry fixed and have no mobile gap. The real oxygen/chemistry path that makes the thin specimen through-coherent belongs to the parent's chemistry integration; the mechanical fixture does not pretend to prove it.

`proposed_accuracy()` supplies numerical trial settings only: divergence 1e-9 /s, traction 0.01 Pa, relative time error 0.01, absolute geometry error 1e-10 m, slope 0.3, strain 0.1, outgoing donor fraction 0.1, quadrature order 4, at most 4000 active-set iterations, 100 inner iterations and penalty rho=200,000 Pa s. These are not experimentally established validity limits, a claimed production tuning or permission to skip an unconverged wait. The parent must measure cost and convergence before choosing retained production solver settings.

The written tests cover the depth integral, signed force, stress-free stiffness joining, Newtonian/Bingham limits, finite-depth vs thin-depth mobility, control fixture matching, solid-state retention, invalid material, bonded-cohort bending compatibility, exact shear/normal-strain integrals, cached-vs-quadrature Hessian agreement and an actual eight-case variational patch table with a conservative tracer gather. The corrected table uses an 8x8x2, 0.4 mm patch and dt=1e-3 s; it asserts resolved growth/decay, exact arrest, compression consumption and the ledger rather than merely checking input labels. The scratch runner is `/home/user/.capy/work/oil-mechanics-run.rs` on the implementation machine; it takes control, lateral sample count, dt, `finite`/`linear`, optional depth count, optional Gauss order and an optional conservative-volume CSV output path. It reports measured outcomes, including projected seed gain and its actual step-doubling error. The integration still owns the actual chemistry thin-film fixture, engine tracer/checkpoint proof, registered gate coverage, canonical ownership and visual/knife behavior.
