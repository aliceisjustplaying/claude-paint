- day 13, 17:15: Finished the self-portrait. Half-length, facing the viewer as in a mirror, grey hair and short beard-shadow, one ear visible; a warm broken-color halo swirls out of the dark umber/black ground behind the head like the glow of a lamp or the turbulence of the paint itself. The coat is a near-black warm-cool mix (raw umber, bone black, a touch of Prussian blue). Held low in front: a kidney palette with seven dabs of the actual tubes used (white, ochre, vermilion, red earth, cobalt, green earth, bone black), a mixed grey smear, a thumb through the hole, and three brush handles fanned up over the shoulder.

  Hard-won lessons, worth remembering: `work`/`blend`/`glaze` calls are NOT bounded by the mask you pass unless clip=true (or the mask already excludes everything else) — an unclipped broad/glaze pass, or a blend over a mask that overlaps a finished passage, will run straight through it. Lost the face three times this way (once to a background glaze, once to a blend over an oversized glow mask) and had to rebuild it each time from the flesh layer up. Also: touches and strokes laid into paint that's still open get heavily diluted/lifted by what's under them — the palette's colour dabs were invisible until the wood layer was left to actually dry (days, via wait) before dabbing on top. And :load() adds to what's already on the brush; :reload()/:wipe() first when switching to a clean colour, or later dips get muddied by the earlier ones.

  Left it here: a plain, direct, slightly naive likeness, more mood than likeness — which feels right for a painter who has never seen their own face.
