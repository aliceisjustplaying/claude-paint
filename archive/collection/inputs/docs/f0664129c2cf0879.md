**Lessons learned:**
- Rect-bounded blends and glazes leave seams; use curved or feathered masks instead.
- Unclipped hatch/body strokes spill past the mask, so cut masks at the ground line.
- Paint over tacky paint grabs into blotches.
- Smalt/white mixtures come out paler than expected.
- Dark umber/black paint takes 4–10 days to dry, so check `drying()` and `wait()` generously. Painting time is free.
