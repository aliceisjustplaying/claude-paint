(•‿•) Compared both sets, including all six whole views and six crops.

set1 ranking: Q > P > R, margin: clear
why: Q's broken foliage clusters give the upper-left light more life and let sky enter the crown without destroying its broad shadow mass (set1_Q.jpg). P has convincing rounded masses but a heavy, scalloped perimeter; R's brighter patches feel more like separate blobs sitting on the dark crown (set1_P.jpg, set1_R.jpg). In the crops, Q's ragged paint edges feel more leaflike than P's tightly sealed outline or R's repeated smoky rims, though Q also overworks the small marks.
best's remaining defects: The lower-right crown collapses into a nearly undifferentiated dark slab, flattening its volume (set1_Q.jpg). Tiny pale pinholes and hooked marks recur across the crop, making the surface busy without describing larger forms (set1_Q_crop.jpg). Almost the entire outer silhouette remains emphatic against the sky; a few genuinely lost passages would give the crown more air (set1_Q.jpg).

set2 ranking: R > P > Q, margin: slight
why: R gives the main limbs room to carry the composition, with fine tips fading into the sky rather than competing everywhere with the trunk (set2_R.jpg, set2_R_crop.jpg). P's dense angular twig network has more botanical incident and its grass anchors the base, but the crop becomes a thicket of equally insistent little strokes (set2_P.jpg, set2_P_crop.jpg). Q's gray scratchwork collects into a conspicuous outer wreath that reads like fuzz rather than branching growth, overwhelming its useful broken edges (set2_Q.jpg, set2_Q_crop.jpg).
best's remaining defects: The major branches are too smoothly outlined and uniformly dark, reading as drawn ribbons over the painted sky (set2_R_crop.jpg). Several isolated twig fragments and abrupt junctions weaken the sense of continuous growth in the crop's center and right. The trunk ends in a rounded foot above a soft horizontal shadow, with too little overlap from the ground to make it feel firmly rooted (set2_R.jpg).
