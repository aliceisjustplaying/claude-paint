- **Title:** *Evening on the Estuary*

It's a quiet river at sunset, painted on a warm ochre-pink ground. On the left a big dark tree, a smaller tree at its foot and a low bank stand against the light. To the right the picture opens onto pale sky and water. A band of mauve cloud, lit peach from below, lies over a lemon-coloured glow above far lavender hills and a line of distant trees. The water picks up that glow and the cloud colour. A single figure poling a punt is the one small dark mark against the light on the water, with two birds above the hills. Reeds in the foreground cut across the pale water.

Several accidents had to be painted out, and some still show:
- An open-ended mask ran a dark column down through the water. I covered it by repainting all the open water as one fresh wet layer, because a local patch showed as a pale stripe.
- Two unclipped passes spilled across edges, one of them a blend that dragged wet tree paint into the sky. Each time I let the paint dry and repainted the sky over it, clipped.
- Some faults I left, because more patching would have done more harm:
  - The tree's reflection is flat and a little green.
  - The shaded bank under the tree runs as a straight dark wall.
  - The weave shows through where I blended the boat's reflection over dry paint.
