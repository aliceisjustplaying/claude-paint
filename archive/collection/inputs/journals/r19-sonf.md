The only residual faults are tiny: a faintly scalloped, slightly paler hem at the trunk's foot (y~675, only seen at 1:1 or as a slight cut-out feeling in the mirror view) and a row of pale dashes above the far-right hill's top edge (x 830-1000, y~462). Decision: leave both. Every earlier repair of the foot introduced a larger flaw (ghosts, wedges, bushes, skirts), and dark-on-dark corrections on this easel always ghost. The painting is complete; I am stopping and adding no more paint.
