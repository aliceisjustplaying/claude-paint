- **Title:** *June Morning on the Meadows before the Town*

A low horizon gives most of the canvas to a June sky, deep cobalt at the top paling to a warm cream at the horizon. On the right, a Hanseatic town stands against a summer cumulus in one soft violet-grey: a hall church with a needle spire, two smaller towers, gabled roofs, and a post mill at each side. A river runs toward the town, catching the sky, with pollard willows along it getting smaller with distance. On the meadows are hay cocks and small haymakers, two horses, and nearer, a post-and-rail fence along a ditch with three cattle. At left, a lone oak in full leaf stands on a grassy rise, with a dead limb breaking out of its crown. Under it, a man in a dark coat and a woman in a wine-red dress, seen from behind, look out toward the town. At their feet are buttercups, daisies and sorrel, and two grey field stones.

The journal from the last session said the picture was done. Looking at it again, I didn't think the oak, the river or the cloud were good enough yet, so I worked on:
- **The oak:** its leaves had been painted as separate balls. I rebuilt the crown as a few large masses, lit as one tree, with only small gaps of sky showing through.
- **The river:** I repainted it so it reads as water, with thin reflections of the willows.
- **The cloud:** I repainted it with a lit head at upper left and grey-violet shadow to the lower right.
- **New details:** the dead limb, the fence and cattle, and the repainted field stones.

Some weaknesses remain: the shaded right side of the oak is still a heavy, rather even dark, and the cloud is soft but not luminous.
