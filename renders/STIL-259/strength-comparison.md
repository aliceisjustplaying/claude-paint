# Strengths of the 39 RGB-only and 15 measured tubes

All 39 RGB-only scalar strengths are unchanged from round 3, 0.3–1.6. All 15 measured scalar strengths are 1.0, without round 3's RGB compensation multipliers. Measured K and S now carry their relative tinting strength. A scalar of 1.0 does not mean equal tinting power.

Actual Palette::pile mixtures in lead white, Euclidean OKLab distance from the same lead white (no raw RGB lerp):

| tubes | count | share | minimum | median | maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RGB-only | 39 | 1% | 0.025715 | 0.069257 | 0.165670 |
| RGB-only | 39 | 10% | 0.082863 | 0.176287 | 0.371211 |
| measured, excluding both whites | 13 | 1% | 0.055162 | 0.136172 | 0.409105 |
| measured, excluding both whites | 13 | 10% | 0.153555 | 0.302172 | 0.639368 |

At 1%, the strongest RGB-only tube is Antwerp blue (0.165670); measured Prussian blue is 0.409105 and carmine lake 0.271461. Measured emerald green is 0.055162, so not every measured tube is stronger than every RGB-only tube. These measurements describe this model, not a calibration of the 39 missing paint spectra.

For the cream probe, ochre 10% × 0.8 contributes 0.08 concentration weight, vermilion 5% × 1.0 contributes 0.05: ochre/vermilion is 1.6:1. In round 3 it was 0.08 versus 0.3385 (1:4.23125). Different K and S still produce different tinting power per unit weight.

The three vermilion grades now share the one measured Vermillion spectrum and scalar strength. Their hiding, stiffness, drying and oil properties retain their catalog values. All 54 per-tube measurements are in strength-comparison.csv.
