377: A rag dipped in spirits (`r:dip`, 0..1) lifts wet paint more readily and
378: reaches farther into the hollows. It works gradually: wipe after wipe
379: takes the paint nearly to the ground. Its folds still leave streaks.
380: Dampness (`r.damp`) halves every three minutes of painting time; a refold
381: turns out a dry part, and another dip wets it again.
382: 
383: What it lifts soaks into the cloth. `r.load` is how loaded the part in use
384: is (0 clean, 1 full): the more loaded, the less it lifts. `r:refold()`
385: turns a cleaner part outward, but no part is cleaner than what has soaked
386: through the whole cloth (`r.soaked`, 0..1) leaves it; `rag()` takes a
387: fresh one. These numbers can be read, not set. Over a mask, `refold=`
388: refolds whenever the load passes that value.
389: 
390: Over a mask, `wipe` lays wipes side by side across it in `angle`'s
391: direction, back and forth, each starting and stopping a little inside the
392: mask. The pad's rim reaches the mask's edge, and in places past it:
393: the rag isn't clipped to the mask. `passes` goes over it again, each time
394: a little turned. `seed=` fixes the cloth's folds.
395: 
396: **What it can't reach.** It lifts only open paint, and less the further
397: the paint has set: paint that is `"setting"` comes away slowly, and paint
398: past its gel point (`"tacky"` or `"dry"` under `drying(x, y)`) doesn't come
399: off at all. Paint laid over a set film lifts down to that film.
