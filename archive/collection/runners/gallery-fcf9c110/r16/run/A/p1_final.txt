**Title:** *Hünengrab im Schnee am Abend* (Dolmen in Snow at Evening)

A snowy rise at dusk, seen against a band of lemon and peach light under a grey-violet sky with a thin crescent moon. A huge dead-topped oak with a crow on its bare leader stands over a small figure seen from behind, who has come up a trail to an ancient stone grave. The grave is a snow-capped capstone on leaning uprights, with a fallen stone and the misty plain showing between them. A broken oak stands on the right, and a ghostly Gothic ruin and fir woods sit in the far haze. Muted dry grasses, a broken stump and a snow-capped boulder hold the cool foreground. The painting has a thin mastic varnish, and the journal is in `notes/journal.md`.

The last stretch had some trouble at the dolmen. A glaze meant to calm an over-strong granite texture ran past its edges, so I repainted the stones opaque and matched the plain around them using small test patches. That rework also got rid of the old round, bridge-like arches. The bottom-right boulder is still a bit schematic, with its snow, shade and rock in stacked bands.
