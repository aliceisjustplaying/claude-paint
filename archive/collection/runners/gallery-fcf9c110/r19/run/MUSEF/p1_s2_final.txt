Morning over a Sea of Fog — Two Bare Trees

A grey dawn above, a warm infinite cream sea of fog, and a dark cliff firm against the light. Two slender bare trees frame the distance — the left fuller, the right lean — with a tiny dark Rückenfigur standing at the centre of the edge, looking out. Two quiet boulders with small grass tufts ground the foreground, and birds cross the luminous void. Painted thin throughout over a warm ground with graphite below, in Friedrich's manner: no impasto, no distant ridge or ruin left, only mist, light, and the watcher.
