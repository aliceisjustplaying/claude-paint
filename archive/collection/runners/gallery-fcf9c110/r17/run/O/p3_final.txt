- **Title:** *Evening on the Estuary, the Punt Coming Home*

It's a quiet estuary at sundown, meant in the tonal spirit of Corot and Constable. A big tree stands dark against the light on the left, over a reedy foreground bank. On the right the sky and water open out, lit by a low glow under a bank of violet clouds with pink undersides. Pale lavender hills and a line of distant trees sit on the far shore. One punter stands poling a small boat on the still water, a single dark mark against the glow's reflection. Two birds cross the light above the hills.

Some weaknesses remain. The tree's reflection is flat and a little too green. The bank under the tree runs as one straight dark band. Where I softened the boat's reflection over dry paint, the canvas weave shows through. I left these alone because more patching would likely do more harm than good.
