- **Title:** *Stoneware Jug with Two Lemons and a Knife*

It's a quiet still life in the manner of Chardin. A cream stoneware jug stands on a stone ledge against a smoky olive wall, lit from the upper left. Two lemons sit beside it, and a knife with a dark wooden handle hangs over the front edge. The jug's shadow side is mottled like salt glaze; it's made of thin umber glazes over dry light paint, blended only within the shadow half. The lemons were repainted in strokes that curve round the form, with pitted-skin stipple, small highlights and contact shadows. A light varnish at the end warmed the whole picture and tied it together.
