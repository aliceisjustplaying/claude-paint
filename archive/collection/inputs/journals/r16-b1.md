- day 1, 09:00: Plan: "Summer Evening on the Elbe Meadows". 71x50 cm, fine linen, red-ochre knifed ground under a brushed lead-white/ochre top. Low horizon ~y440. Sky after sunset: lemon/peach glow at the horizon rising through pale green to smalt/cobalt at the top, thin strata of violet cloud low, a waxing crescent moon upper right. Flat meadows with sky-reflecting pools, far blue hills, a small town with church towers right of centre. A stag-headed oak left of centre (dead limbs above a living crown), dark against the glow. Dark foreground bank with grasses, a path; two small figures seen from behind at right of centre looking into the glow. Method after Friedrich: graphite drawing, very thin lay-in, thin layers, stippled gradations, details last.
- day 2, 08:33: Sky laid in thinly in bands (smalt/cobalt+white at top, pale smalt, a greenish white, chrome-yellow/vermilion glow at the horizon), blended wet with the badger in several passes. Bands still a bit stepped; will correct later by stippling over the dry lay-in, as he did. Letting it set overnight before the land.
- day 6, 09:42: Oak built: trunk and boughs as spline ribbons, gnarled with noise; secondary branches grown by hand-seeded crooked recursion; dead stag-horn leader and two dead side limbs left bare above the crown. Foliage stippled into clumps (branch tips + hand-placed masses) - reads too green and scalloped, needs warmer darker olive, sky holes, lighter rims. Distance: far tree lines, town with a tall spire and a second tower, poplars, all violet-grey in the glow - good. Foreground went wrong: bands of warm/cool with a straight seam, then blending with rect masks left more seams, smeared the root flare and dissolved the path. Lesson: no rect-edged blends over body passages. Letting it dry to repaint the bank opaquely.
- day 14, 15:43: Sky: stippled smalt/cobalt over the dry lay-in to even the zenith, fused with a badger; a band of fine dots remains in the transition - I keep it, it is the texture of his skies. Thin evening strata near the glow, dragged soft with the blender. Young crescent moon upper right, lit toward the sunken sun. River got dark reedy far banks and faint violet reflections. Mistake: meadow "mown strips" with a filbert 5 unclipped - crude contrasty strokes across trunk, river and bank. Blended the meadow hard while open; it now reads as a smooth, broadly modelled field. To repair after drying: trunk, far river reach, bank crest.
- day 16, 18:45: Meadow: tried a finishing layer of short hatch touches - came out as cobbles with hard-edged noise islands. Blended it with long ruler-straight horizontal badger passes while open: now a smooth horizontal field with soft darker hollows, which is closer to how he treats a plain at this distance: smooth gradation, with interest carried by particular things (willows, hay, figures), not texture.

The shawl picked up red from the wet dress; to restate. Next: foreground (grasses, crest, plants, a boulder), mist over the meadow, trunk glaze.
