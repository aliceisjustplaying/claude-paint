- **Title:** *Summer Evening on the Meadows before the Town*

It's a wide, flat Baltic plain late on a summer day, with the sun low on the left. The horizon sits low, so the smalt-and-cobalt sky takes three fifths of the canvas and warms to cream at the horizon. A small town with three brick church towers stands on the skyline between two post mills and rows of woods. A man and a woman are seen from behind on a worn footpath, looking toward the town, their shadows falling long to the right.

Across the meadows there are:
- a row of pollard willows along a ditch that reflects the sky, with a stork on the near bank
- grazing horses and cows, and a post-and-rail fence crossing a band of cloud shadow
- haycocks with rows of cut hay, and a hay wagon with a man forking hay up to another on the load
- a far-off shepherd with his flock and dog, and a few birds flying home

In the foreground a granite boulder sits in the grass among small flowers.

Some parts took several tries, all recorded in the journal:
- **The boulder** kept coming out too light or muddy while wet. It only came right once it had dried and I built it up with thin glazes.
- **The ditch** was first a stark white diagonal line across the whole plain. I painted most of it out and kept only the stretch by the willows.
- **The red dress glaze** ran under the woman's shawl, so I repainted the shawl once it was dry.

Once everything was touch-dry I laid a light mastic varnish over the whole, so the colours sit a little warmer, as his pictures look today.
