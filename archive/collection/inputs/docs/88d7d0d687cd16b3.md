**Lessons learned (in the journal):**
- A badger blend over a whole small object averages away the modelling. Blending a partial zone leaves the zone's outline, so blend whole fields only (the whole-wall blend worked).
- Lead-white mixes dominate wet-into-wet: darks laid into wet pale come out pale.
- `clip` gives hard edges even at soft mask ramps.
- `work()` passes with a soft mask plus a threshold lay scaly separate dabs.
- Pure glazes (umber/green earth/black) go on far too dark. A clean brush with `b:wipe(1)` after each stroke lifts wet paint effectively.
- A form's value is low outside its solid, so scaffold masks spill beyond it.
- What works: hand-planned `b:stroke` marks following the form, lights and darks as separate strokes, laid over dry underpaint without blending.
- Drying: about 2–3 days (`wait`) for these layers to reach "dry".
