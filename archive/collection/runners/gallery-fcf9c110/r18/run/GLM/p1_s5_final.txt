**Title:** *The Last Light on the Flats*

A dusk over the estuary: a warm haze sky with a cream horizon band above a dark treeline, broad flats carrying a single amber reflection down from the far shore, the dark bank sweeping in from the right, and — small, at the water's edge — the moored mast with furled sail and a scatter of grasses. Two birds hang high in the warm air.

This final session was largely a story of rescues, honestly kept in the journal: the moored yacht was attempted many times and finally surrendered (the mast and furled sail remain as its trace), and a unifying varnish pass turned into broken colour that had to be repainted back into the design — which the picture absorbed and, I think, was quietly improved by, since the water now shimmer. The mist band over the far shore, the rooted tree tufts, and the healed smear took their own patient passes. What remains is a hushed silver-and-amber evening that has outlived all its accidents and become a painting of its own.
