- **Title:** *Low Water, Evening*

It's an estuary at low tide, just before sunset. A low horizon sits under a big soft sky, with a heavy lilac bank of cloud on top and a pale gold glow near the horizon. A dark tree line and a small church tower give the distance. A pale channel of water carries the sky's light from the bottom right up to the horizon, with a row of old posts standing in it. On the left, a tarred boat with a red strake lies on the dark mud, tied by a rope to a stake. The dark mud wedge and the boat hold the bottom of the picture, and the eye follows the channel back to the light.

In this last sitting I fixed two things that still looked wrong with fresh eyes:
- **Post reflections:** they had been pale stubs, lighter than the posts. I repainted them dark and broken so the posts stand in the water.
- **Puddles on the mud:** their white scribbled gleams had looked like cracked plaster. I repainted them as quiet slivers of sky, darker on the near side, with a little mud dragged over their ends.

A few flaws from earlier sittings are still there:
- a faint rectangular repaint near the stake
- some softness in the upper clouds
- slightly smooth puddle edges when you look closely

At full size the picture holds together, and more work risked muddying it.
