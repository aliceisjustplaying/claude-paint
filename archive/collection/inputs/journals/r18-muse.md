- day 1, 09:00: Opening the studio. Plan: a late-afternoon marsh landscape — pale luminous sky, low sun glow, distant tree line, still water with hand-painted reflections, dark reeds in the foreground. Warm light against cool shadow. I will build it in layers: ground, sky, distant hills/trees, water, foreground, reeds and finishing touches, letting paint set between passages.
- day 1, 09:44: Sky lay-in: cool blue top into pale middle and warm golden horizon. Broad handling with fill, broken color showing. Next I will soften the bands together, then model clouds and sun glow while the paint is still open.
- day 1, 10:23: Clouds came out as flat ovals and blending left halo rings where blended meets unblended sky. I will knit the sky back together with fresh broad paint, then repaint clouds as irregular hand-drawn bodies with soft edges and minimal blending.
- day 1, 11:04: Far shore and tree masses are in. The yellow below the trees is still sky paint; next is the water, which will meet the trees at their bases and carry a hand-painted sun glitter path — no mirrored geometry, all shapes drawn by eye.
- day 1, 13:44: Hatch sparkle came out as a dense rectangular carpet with hard edges — far too regular and heavy. I will veil it back with thin water paint, leaving only a ghost of shimmer, then re-sparkle sparsely with soft-edged stipple in the sun shaft alone.
- day 2, 00:18: Close look shows the first stalks dissolved into the wet reed bases and came out as ghosts. After an overnight rest the paint is tacky, so stalks should now sit on top. I will restalk with fuller loads and add seed heads.
- day 2, 01:15: Foreground now reads as mottled marshy ground and the reed beds sit in it. Next: seat the far islands in mist, add a few distant birds, and work sunlit grass strokes into the foreground.
- day 2, 03:06: The far islands have become overworked rings — too even and symmetrical. I will wipe the distance back to a simple hazy shore and repaint it once, as a varied treeline rather than four matching islands.
- day 2, 03:56: Finished: evening marsh with far treeline, shimmering water and sun path, reed beds in a mottled foreground, four birds high. The paint taught me restraint — large soft veils over small crisp drawing, thin mottle layers over heavy ovals, and letting tacky paint hold the final stalks. I judge it complete.
- day 2, 03:56: Reopened day 2: reviewed whole canvas, squint, and close crops of water/treeline and foreground reeds. Values hold — pale sky, dark treeline band, luminous water with sun path, mottled foreground with reed beds and birds. Handling reads as hand-laid paint throughout. I judge it complete as an evening marsh; saving now.
