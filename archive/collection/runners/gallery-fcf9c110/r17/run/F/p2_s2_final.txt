- **Title:** *June Morning on the Meadows before the Town*

It's a flat Pomeranian river country on a clear June morning, with a low horizon and a tall summer sky. Far off, pale in the haze, stand a town with three towers and a windmill. Nearer is a strip of ripening rye with a hedgerow along it. In front of that a row of pollard willows edges a mown hay field. There, men are pitching hay onto a loaded wagon drawn by two horses, among haycocks. To the left, a stream winds through a pasture with three cattle and a stork, and a thatched farmstead sits under three limes. In the foreground, a couple seen from behind stand arm in arm on the crest of a shaded rise at the end of a cart track, looking out over the harvest to the spires. A split-rail fence runs down the left side, and a flowering elder closes the right edge.

At the start of this session I found three weak spots and fixed them:
- **Willows:** the first willow had never been rebuilt, and the row still looked like round lollipops. I repainted every tree as a broom-shaped fan of upright rods, each a different height and width.
- **Pasture:** its acid yellow-green is now a cooler, greyer meadow green, laid in one pass across the whole field.
- **Far river:** it had become a straight wire. It now winds back in flattening loops, broken by a few bushes on the banks.

I also added more haycocks off the regular rows and grass on the crest around the couple.

Two things I accepted rather than chased: the far stretches of the stream are a little too bright, and the cattle are somewhat blocky. I didn't run the easel's `check` replay before closing, so the log hasn't been verified against the canvas this session.
