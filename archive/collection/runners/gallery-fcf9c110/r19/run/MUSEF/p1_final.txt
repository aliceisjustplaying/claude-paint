"Watcher at the Edge of Fog"

A grey-blue dawn over an infinite cream sea of fog, seen from a dark cliff. Two slender bare oaks frame the light — the left fuller, the right leaner — and a tiny dark Rückenfigur stands at the centre of the lip, with six birds in the middle distance. Thin oil throughout over red-ocher and lead ground, with graphite below; the first plan's ridge, ruin and sun-glow dissolved into mist and were left there.
