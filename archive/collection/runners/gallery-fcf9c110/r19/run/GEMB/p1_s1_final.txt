# Still Life with Clay Pitcher, Quince, and Knife

Oil on primed linen, 500 × 400 mm.

A rustic earthenware pitcher and a ripe golden quince rest upon a dressed limestone table against a deep, velvety chiaroscuro wall. Lit by a cool, raking north light from the upper left, the terracotta body of the pitcher glows in earthy red and yellow ochre, crowned by an amber glaze highlight and an arched loop handle that anchors the vessel's vertical mass. Beside it, the quince bulges with organic, knobby presence, turning from sunlit gold into a soft umber core shadow beneath its delicate woody stem. In the foreground, an old steel knife lies diagonally along the stone slab, its turned dark wood handle projecting over the front edge to cast a shadow down the vertical face of the ledge and draw the viewer directly into the picture's quiet, contemplative space.
