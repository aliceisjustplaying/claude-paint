# Actual palette strengths, round 5

All 39 RGB-only scalar strengths are unchanged from round 4 (0.3–1.6). The 15 measured tubes now use their calibrated constant weights, 0.654–1.059; these are not concentration-dependent parameters. The three vermilion grades still share one measured paint. No strength measurements were invented for RGB-only paints.

At 1% in lead white, the 39 RGB-only tubes have median OKLab distance 0.044881 and range 0.005868–0.126054. The 13 measured tubes other than the two whites have median 0.116857 and range 0.028939–0.385599. These ranges overlap. The largest RGB-only trace is Antwerp blue; the largest measured trace is Prussian. The original raw-RGB trace guard does not cover this measured-palette route.

Cream still uses white .85, ochre .10 and vermilion .05. Their effective linear weights are 0.555688, 0.080000 and 0.049460, respectively. Ochre/vermilion is 1.6175:1, versus 1.6:1 in round 4.
